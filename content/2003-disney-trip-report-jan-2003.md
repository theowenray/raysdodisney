Disney Trip Report Jan. 2003

Drove down! Lisa: 32 ( almost 33) Alan: 33 ( almost 34) Olivia: 12 Owen: 3 and a half.

This trip was interesting. We did have a good time. I have yet to go and not have a good time. However, this is the 1st time we had all driven down since 1994! And it is the 1st time we have driven far with Owen. This is also the trip that almost wasn’t going to happen many many times.

We had thought of going at our favorite time of year which is Nov./thanksgiving but Grandpa Edwards was really sick and I didn’t want to be gone. Not just in case something happened to him but also because I just knew it was going to be his last holiday. Truly I thought he would make it till Christmas but turns out I was both wrong and right. It was his last holiday as he passed away on Dec. 7th. This was the hardest thing I have ever been through and I am still not over it even though it has been almost 2 years. I will also never ever forget him taking my hand that day at Thanksgiving and patting it and saying “ we’re had a lot of good ones together, kid” It was as if he knew it was his last holiday too. (I of course ran to the bathroom and cried! ) There are so many things that I wish I had done differently that last year, months, days, and especially last hours. There is nothing I can do about it now but I feel I must add all of this in to get the whole “feel” for this trip. As if this weren’t enough, Grandma Agney also passed away in Jan. just a couple days before we left. It was a “should we go or shouldn’t we” for a couple of weeks before we left. Then we decided to go and on the night before we left we are at the funeral home for grandma’s visitation and Mustang our dog passed away. Alan and Olivia were home and hadn’t left yet for the funeral home. They took him out to potty and he just came in and had a seizure on the kitchen floor and his little heart gave out. They came and got me and we came home to say goodbye to him. This is also right up there with the hardest thing I ever had to do. With all the things going on that week he had been neglected. I was going to the nursing home a few times, packing and getting the house ready for the trip etc. and just can’t really remember spending much time with him that week. Oh, the guilt! Mustang was just over 13 years old and had a heart condition and there really aren’t words to try and sum up what he meant to us and especially to me. So I won’t even try. He was just the best little dog. I am adding all of this because it just wasn’t a typical trip at all! Once again we thought should we go? All the bags were packed and we decided to go. Mustang would be buried the next day by mom and dad while we were on the road driving down to Florida. Oh, the guilt! But I wouldn’t have gotten through being there in person so it was probably a good thing we left.

I will also add that I was in a state of depression that had started when grandpa passed away and didn’t leave for months to come. There was nonstop crying at night and very little sleeping at night too. It is truly shocking that I have a trip report of this trip because I didn’t think I had written one at all. I was surprised when I found it. I have told Alan many times that this trip didn’t count because it is all a blur. I don’t remember much of it like I do so many other trips. Except the cold part!! (and maybe the long drive) This one is mostly just foggy. As I read through it I see that we did have fun so I am glad that I actually wrote some of it down since I was in such a daze!

Now for the fun part……………… Sat. Jan. 18th 2003

Up at 6:00am (did I say fun part??) Left home at 7:15am and it is 11 degrees in Shelbyville! We are at the Tennesse state line at 11:20am! Owen was wide awake till 10am and then slept for 1 hour. Lib did a LITTLE homework and fell asleep too. Owen asked twice if we were in California yet? Stopped in Clarksville at 11:25 till 11:50 everyone pee’d and filled up the gas tank. ($1.39 a gal!! $22.00) Alan hooked up the laptop so the kids are now watching Peter Pan. Entering Nashville at 12:15 there are a lot of cars off the side of the interstate between Clarksville and Nashville in the snow. The roads are completely clear now but days before there was a bad storm. We are taking the long way to Orlando to avoid the mountains because of snow and ice. (and mom!) Alabama state line at 1:30. Kid’s movie is over lib is doing homework and Owen is fighting sleep!

Stopped at 2:10 in Cullman Al. other side of Decatur Al. and ate at Arby’s for $16.00 also wasted 20 minutes messing with the laptop because it is now not working AT ALL!! Alan can’t fix it either! Back on road at 3:00!!

Stopped again in Montgomery at a K-mart about 5pm for Owen to pee and so Alan could look at hot wheels. Back in car at 5:25. Outside of Troy we got gas $24.00 ($1.41 a gal) COULD have done this in Montgomery but…… well……… we won’t go there! Lets just say that we haven’t had a driving trip in a long time and didn’t think of it!! Jeez!

Dothan Al. at 7:05pm. Fairfield Inn! Thank God!! Got our room and alan made up for the forgetting to get gas thing by getting us a king bed and sofa sleeper! Now here is where it gets stupid! (besides the gas thing!) We took stuff to the room and then GOT BACK IN THE CAR!! And went down the road to find the barnhills buffet. This took 20 minutes to find! We had just passed it. How could we not be able to find it?! Ate at 7:45 pm! And you know it was good and hot and fresh at that time of the evening right! (yuck!) $26.00 for dinner. Back to room and all asleep at 10pm!

Sunday, Jan. 19th 2003

I got up at 6:25 and showered. (are we having fun YET?) Then alarm went off at 6:45. Yep, that is right I was up BEFORE the alarm! Down for free breakfast at 7:30 and on the road at 8am. (Alan is giving me crap for not flying! And I am telling him it could be worse I could be my mother and get us all up at 2 or 3am! ) 8:25am florida state line. WooHoo! This is when you wish you were just going to Pensacola! Tallahassee at 9:35. Int. 75 at 11:10am. WooHoo gotta love the interstate! We stopped at rest area right at the interstate for a quick potty break and let Owen run and chase bubbles for a few minutes. Then we stopped for gas again. ($22.00, $1.64 a gal., which by the way I was not happy with at all!) We are now hoping Owen who has been awake ALL morning will SLEEP at least for a few minutes!

Gainsville at 12:00. Orlando at 1:20!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!! Holiday inn Family Suites (HIFS) at 1:35!! We are using the Buy one nite get the 2nd nite free coupon! Love that coupon. It was 99.99 a night for the night we paid for though so it works out to $50 a night. But with the free hot breakfast that helps and it is 2 bedrooms too. We used one of these coupons years ago I think it was 1996? And stayed at a not as nice and new Holiday inn for $59 a night and got the 2nd nite free! Now that was a deal. Of course not in the best of Orlando/Disney area’s and no free breakfast but we were fairly new to this and didn’t know any different!

We checked in and unloaded then while I unpacked everything Alan went to Walmart to buy the hopper passes that we needed. The kids loved the room. We had a small living room with sofa and chair. There was a mini-kitchen area. Just a table and chairs, small fridge, microwave, sink and counter top area. There were some over head cabinets to store things in. There was also one good sized bathroom and a bedroom for us with a king bed, closet, TV, and make up table with lights. The kids had a room with bunk beds, a kids sized table and chairs, and their own TV. They played in their room while I worked. We had 2 phone calls about our credit card I guess the problem was that we checked in to the hotel and then went to buy passes and the credit card hadn’t seen that much action in a long time and they thought it was stolen! While I am glad they were looking out for us in case of a stolen card it did make for a pain in the butt proving that it really was US! (Oddly one of the questions they asked me on the phone was the name of my family pet! Well I had to tell them Mustang and then I got all upset about that! When Alan got back I had to tell him about that!)

Alan was back and we were all done unpacking and decided to go down to the lobby and eat dinner/lunch. Well all they had was fast food and we wanted to eat at the buffet. It didn’t open till 5pm so we had to wait and hour. We went to the game room and walked all around the hotel exploring. They claimed to have free mini-golf but it was pretty sad. Back to lobby for dinner. $37.00 including the 15% tip that they added to the bill automatically! Glad we noticed that before we left another tip! Meal was not cheap but it was the only food we had paid for all day long and at least it was a buffet. Plus we paid $26.00 last night at that barnhills and it was really bad! At least this was fresh and hot.

Got in the car and drove over to the outlet store that is close by. The 1st one was closed since it was a Sunday evening. But the 2nd one was open. I got a watch and a snowglobe. Lib got a Tinkerbell t-shirt and Owen got a light up buzz toy. Back to the hotel and down to play bingo at 8pm. We ate the free popcorn and played. Owen got bored and Alan took him to the room at about 8:40. Lib and I stayed for the karaoke till 10pm! She liked watching it but wouldn’t get up and do it. It was very fun to watch the others though. Back to the room, into PJ’s and bed at about 11pm.

Worst: The credit card thing and the guy asking my about my pet.

Favorite: That we made it in one piece. That we liked the hotel and the room.

Up at 7:00am! (are we having fun YET?) Down to free breakfast at 8:15. They have a free hot breakfast and we got our “monies” worth of that! They had scrambled eggs, potatoes and each day they would rotate between bacon, ham, and sausage for meats. Also between French toast or pancakes. Then they would have cereal, toast, bagels and all of that too.

We got to MGM at 9:15am. Bad part about not staying onsite was having to pay $7.00 parking. I prefer free parking!! ($7.00 X 4 parking days = $28.00!) Or riding the busses but the busses from here aren’t an option as they don’t run very often and you can’t come and go when you want like on Disney property.

They went to Rock-n-Roller coaster and Tower of Terror 1st and got fast passes. Owen and I waited for them and he had himself a meltdown cause he wanted to RIDE something NOW! They showed back up and we went to find Buzz to get a picture. Then we got a picture made of all 4 of us. Not with buzz but where they put in different backgrounds. We picked the one that was the “4 parks one world thing” because I have a picture frame to match it and I also was wearing my t-shirt that matched it! Turns out that the t-shirt isn’t even noticeable in the pic because of how we are standing but oh well. ($12.95 for pic)

Kids went to play in the honey I shrunk the audience playground for a few minutes. Owen was afraid of it because it is so huge! Alan got a couple of work phone calls!! This really bugs the crap out of me! ONE WEEK OFF PEOPLE just one week! We had to stand around while he fixed some life threatening emergency at work that apparently only he can do!! (I could understand this if the man were a brain surgeon but give me a break!!)

From here we went over to One Man’s Dream. I really love this, the story of Walt’s life and how he came up the idea of making the parks and all that went into it. All the props, drawings, and his personal stuff to look at. It is just so sad that he died at such a young age and didn’t get to see ALL of it. Yes, I cried during the movie portion of it. Of course that is not unusual lately!

It is now time for them to use the fast passes from this morning. Owen and I went to see the bear in the big blue house show. He wasn’t all that impressed. It would help if he actually watched the bear cartoons! This kids watches plenty of cartoons and yet not this!

Owen and I met them on the main road and got my watch figured out. I was having trouble with it. Then it was noon so we decided to leave for awhile. Back to HIFS, we ate lunch because kids eat free in the food court with a paying adult! And then swam till 2:15. Back to MGM at 3:00 and sat down for the parade. Found a good spot that nobody else had wanted. Wow we are so lucky! But wait….. the sun is blinding us so bad we can’t see a thing! Perhaps this could be why no one else was sitting here?

As soon as the parade went past Al and lib ran down for more fast passes. And we all walked over to who wants to be a millionaire! After we got seated we gave owen a small sucker to keep him quiet but a mean CM came over and yelled at us that he couldn’t have it in there! (very undisney) Alan ended up taking Owen out before it was over he hated sitting still and listening to the questions. Ahhhh, do we feel bad that poor Alan had to take him out and didn’t get to see the rest of the show? NO, we do not, after all I am with Owen while he and lib ride the coasters!

After this they of course left me and owen again! I went and bought owen and I a warm pretzel and cheese to share but while I ordered and paid and then turned around to hand it to him he was……….asleep! So I stopped my urge to eat it all and only ate half and saved the rest for him and lib to share.

Once Al and lib showed up Owen was still asleep and we had to decide what to do. It was about 5:00-5:30 so we took the slow boat to epcot to see if we could ride test track and eat somewhere at one of the countries in World Showcase. Well this is the slowest boat in the world! But Owen slept the whole time even through the boat horns going off. We were hungry and lib and I ordered sweet and sour chicken and took it over to Mexico where alan was eating. While we were eating Owen woke up and sat up in his stroller! Just kind of popped up! It was funny because it was dark outside now and we didn’t know he was awake! This kid had been asleep for 2 hours and all we had done was take the boat to epcot and ordered food! Jeez! Owen actually ate some sweet and sour chicken without any sauce of course. We just told him it was nuggets and I guess it is, sort of.

We walked over to Test track and the wait was 25min. but the fastpass machine was closed so me and lib went. But we did the single rider so we wouldn’t have to wait as long. I hate sitting by strangers but it was much faster. And speaking of faster did they speed test track up or is it just me? WOW! It goes so fast that your teeth feel like they must have bugs in them. You have that plastered smile and your teeth are dry it feels weird. And doesn’t look pretty in the picture either.

Next it was Alan’s turn to go with her. Lib makes out quite well at Disney, she gets to ride everything twice. Once with me and then with Alan, well of course just the things that Owen isn’t big enough for and that I am brave enough for. Once we were all done we realized it was 8pm and that our car is still parked at MGM, which closed at 7pm. So the slow boat is no longer running to take us back over to get our car! Hmmmm how are we going to do this? At the international gateway they told us to go to the Boardwalk hotel and tell the Downtown Disney bus driver to take us to MGM. They said that is what the driver is instructed to do. So off we went but, the driver at the boardwalk acted like he had never heard of such a thing and was not happy! He just shrugged his shoulders and said ok, get on. So at least he did it. But I will say the CM’s at the international gateway acted like this has happened before and it was what we were supposed to do. Well the bus of course dropped us up front at the hotel bus drop off area and the van was WAY over in the parking lot. With no trams running Alan went out to get the car and we walked over to another area so he could pick us up. There was not a person in sight anywhere it was really spooky!

Got in the van and it was 8:55, we were back at the hotel parking lot at 9:00 for karaoke! We watched till 10:30! Then up to room and into PJ’s and kids went to bed. Alan and I stayed up for the 11pm news to see the 5 day forcast. (there is no news at 10pm, I hate that!) The weather does not look good for the rest of the week!!

Worst: paying to park, the mean lady who wouldn’t let Owen have his sucker in the millionaire show. And Alan’s work phone calls. Trying to get back to our car. You don’t have these problems if you stay onsite and use the Disney busses.

Favorite: Walt’s show One Man’s Dream, riding test track. The fact that we didn’t have to pay a cab to get back to our car.

Owen woke me up at 8-8:30 and I got up with him. Alan got up a few minutes later. Showered, dressed and down to breakfast at 10:00am. Sausage and pancakes today and Owen ate a lot this morning! Went to game room for 15 minutes or so then out front for the free train rides. Alan had a couple of work calls and he is waiting on UPS to deliver a part for his laptop so the kids can watch it on the way home. At 11 we put our suits on and went down to pool. Alan came with us but kept leaving to check on his UPS package. Called home from the pool. UPS finally showed up at 12:30 so he went to room and messed with that for an hour! Me and kids came up to get snacks for the pool and he came back down with us. We rented an inflatable alligator at the pool for $5.00 an hour. (only got 1 hour) And we got in with them to play.

When we left here there was so much traffic around those downtown Disney hotels Alan said don’t ever plan a trip where we are staying at one of those hotels! It was just a wreck waiting to happen. Back to the room about 8-8:30 and Owen had fallen asleep in the car and gotten a short cat nap so now he was awake again! So at 9pm we went down for karaoke and Owen went in his Woody PJ’s. Lib and Owen (in his jimmies) got up on stage and sang twinkle twinkle little star. Then lib asked some girl she met to sing a song with her. So they sang dancing queen and we stayed till 10:45pm!!! I was expecting something stupid but it was really good. Looked just like the stuff they charge $10.00 for in the parks.

Worst: That the meal at bahama breeze was so expensive. We thought it would be almost free with the gift card. Alan spending forever waiting on the package and fixing the laptop! Favorite: Well I will say the meal at bahama breeze was very good! Swimming at the pool in Jan. and Owen singing karaoke in his Woody PJ’s!

Oh, I thought of something else…….. taking forever to get out of that park! An hour and a half from closing till we got to the car. Some of which was because we sat and thought we would wait for the crowds to leave only to find out they weren’t leaving!

Do you think it can go from 28 to 75 degrees in 2 hours? No, not gonna happen!

Down for breakfast and to the van around 10am. Freezing outside! Now at home 30 degrees doesn’t feel this bad I don’t know why but this cold just hurts! We brought beach towels to use as blankets! We head to MGM and Al and lib go off to ride the 2 favorites and luckily they are both indoors! However, Owen and I are stuck sitting outside on a bench waiting for them. As we walked away from the bench I turned my head back to see if I forgot anything and didn’t see it. But that was the last place we ever saw it! I was not happy!

We went down by Rock n roller coaster to wait and saw the green army men. Owen was afraid of them. Then we took a potty break just to warm up in the bathroom. Took our time in there since it was nice and warm. When we came out we saw Al and lib were going to ride again!!! They had gotten fast passes and just rode without using them since there was no line but now they were going back to use the passes! I was not pleased to hear this cause we were bored and cold! But we heard that they were lining people up for a shortened beauty and the beast show so we went to go do that. While we were waiting this is when we noticed the missing buzz! Owen started crying and it wasn’t pretty. I thought I might shed a tear or two also, as I was not having a good day! But as they say the show must go on, and once it started he shut up.
