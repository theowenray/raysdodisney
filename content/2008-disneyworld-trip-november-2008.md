Plans! It seems like a lot of our trips start with me saying that we weren’t going to go and yet then we go but this one we REALLY weren’t going to go. We had said that since it was Olivia’s senior year we wouldn’t be taking a trip this year. But ever since they started having free dining I have always wished and hoped that they would offer free dining during the late fall or winter instead of just during hurricane season. So one September afternoon I was minding my own business and was reading online when I read that Disney was offering Free Dining in November! And you could book it starting TOMORROW!! It was only available for certain dates in November but still it was the 1st time they have ever offered this! I got on the phone instantly and I called mom at work and told her. She was excited but hesitant and she had a lot of questions! Well I was already online looking for frequent flier seats and for flights for her and dad I was picking out dates and flights already! I also called Alan during all of that to tell him and he said he didn’t know if he could be gone at that time because of something at work going on so I told him that we were going with or without him so figure it out. I’m sure he thought I was joking but I wasn’t! By the time mom was off work I had a list of flights and a schedule all worked out I had never worked so hard in a two hour time frame in my life. My head was spinning this was only three months away and there was a lot to be planned and done. The other thing I was doing was calling and trying to see if any of the restaurants were available because having free dining with only three months notice could mean that everywhere we like to eat is already booked up! Well that evening was a total blur and the deal couldn’t be booked until the next morning so I have to have the whole thing figured out that evening so I could get up early and call and book the room and also get the flights. Then I would also need to call for restaurant reservations because as soon as everyone starts booking this in the morning that will be the next thing they do. Then I thought to myself that I could be tricky and call tonight and book the restaurants ahead of time because you don’t have to have a hotel reservation to book them so that is what I did. I booked a whole week’s worth of places for a party of six and fell asleep that night feeling so smug and thinking that I was one step ahead of everyone else.

The next morning I woke up at 6am and called being put on hold. I knew that would happen it can take an hour or two to get through sometimes when deals like this are released. I knew that the phone lines were very busy right now but I needed to rethink the schedule before calling and changing the meals if it was just going to be the four of us. Honestly in looking back I should have just went ahead and called and then while I sat on hold forever I could have gotten our new plans all figured out during that time.
