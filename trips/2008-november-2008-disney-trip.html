November 2008 Disney Trip!

Wow this trip was actually perfect. The only thing that would have made it any better would be if it had been warmer!! There were a few days it was VERY COLD! But if you exclude the weather it was a GREAT trip! It was 8 nights of non stop GO GO GO! And it was FUN FUN FUN!

As I start this trip report off I will say that we weren’t going this year. No really we weren’t! Olivia is a SENIOR and we didn’t want her to miss school etc etc. But every year for the past 4 or so I have read about the free dining plan which is only offered for FREE during the hurricane season of Aug./Sept. and it is so HOT then we never want to go and in the last 2 years they have even narrowed it down to only Aug. But this year with the economy going to pot there are less people traveling so in late September when I read that they were offering FREE dining for one week in Oct., two weeks in Nov. and 5 days in Dec. I called mom immediately! I said this is too good to be true you HAVE to go then I got online looking for frequent flier flights and though we usually book these at least 6 months ahead of time the fact that less people are traveling meant there were still some flights out there. I also looked up flights for mom and dad. I called Alan during all of this and told him what was going and that we were going in Nov. because Oct. was too soon and Dec. it’s only good for 5 days which isn’t enough time. He told me he would have to check because there is big work stuff going on in Nov. but I said well with or without you WE are going and I think he knew I was serious.

Well this free dining package couldn’t be booked till the next morning so I spent the rest of the day and all night dealing with extra fast planning. I put our FF seats on hold chose dates based on that and then looked into flights for mom and dad and also had to plan our meals because as soon as people book their hotel they will book up all the restaurants. I was in a frenzied panic! I ran back and forth to moms a couple times with info and never mentioned meal plans because that would seem so trivial to them and flights were what they needed to worry about right now.

Finally sometime after dinner I got on the phone and called to make all my dining reservations because even though I couldn’t book the package yet doesn’t mean I can’t book the meal reservations! Now tomorrow I will be one step ahead and will only have to sit on hold for the package and not for this too. I was pretty pleased with myself for thinking of this.

So the next morning the alarm went off and while I was making sack lunches I was already on hold. When I was done with the lunches I got on the laptop at the kitchen table to check my Disney site and see that everyone else was on hold but with a few people reporting that they had gotten through. Those that had gotten through were also reporting that Pop Century was not available for this promotion! That really ticked me off because in none of my planning last night had I even thought about what hotel it was just assumed it would be Pop! As I continued on hold mom walked over and gave me a piece of paper since she couldn’t email me as our email and main computer were out of order! She asked who I was on the phone with and seemed surprised that it was with Disney! I guess she didn’t take me seriously when I said I’m calling 1st thing in the morning! So she leaves and I read her paper which says that her and dad aren’t going they talked it over and I can’t remember why right now but at the same time that I get done reading this paper there is a woman on the phone saying can I help you??? I am thinking well CRAP! I have written out in front of me what all I need to tell her. 6 people, 2 rooms, etc etc. and I had no time to mentally adjust to finding out they didn’t want to go before booking so I told her OUR info and booked 8 nights for the four of us and got it at Pop Century! Yeah to that!

Hang up the phone happy that I got it booked and where I wanted it at but slightly ticked at mom because now I have to call and change all our meals! Well 1st I called Alan who was still driving to work and told him I was on hold 40 minutes and finally booked it and mom and dad aren’t going so he had better figure out if he can go or not otherwise it’s just me and the kids. And as much as I’d hate to do it…………..I would! Haha!

Then I posted online telling others that I had booked at Pop to give others hope then I wasn’t in the mood to sit on hold for another 40 minutes or who knows how long so I thought I’d wait till after I showered and ate breakfast. Then around 9:30 am I called to make dining plans for 4 only to find out that EVERYONE had spent the past 2 hours booking tons of stuff. I couldn’t get Chef Mickey’s at all not for any of the 8 nights and same with O’hanas! So I booked some other stuff and decided not to cancel O’hanas and just show up and say that 2 of our 6 couldn’t make it and hope they would take us. Those Disney restaurants aren’t big on that stuff the tables either seat 4 or 8 and they make reservations for every table to be full all night and take it very seriously. But it was only for that one place so we’d hope for the best.

*Alan called that afternoon and said he could go and I said somehow I knew you would! He can’t let me go to Walmart without him he’s not about to let me go to Disney without him! Haha!

So there I was all done with all my planning. Just did the stuff that normally takes me a few months in less then 24 hours! Holy Crap! I had booked the hotel, had the flights on hold, booked the meals, and even booked the rental car because our flight would be into Tampa and out of Orlando so we would need a car one way. Oh and I had also looked up all the park hours for each day so that I could coordinate all the meals to go along with that. Gee now what am I going to do?

Well I did end up changing the rental car reservation about 5 times before the trip because I would find better rates. But other than that, nothing.

So with the flights that we have the kids will miss 6 days of school we fly out at the crack of dawn on Friday Nov. 14th and fly home late in the evening on Sat. Nov. 22nd. And truth be told I was bummed that it wasn’t long enough. The last couple trips were over Thanksgiving time and we were gone 10days and the kids only missed 5 days of school. So 8 nights didn’t seem like long enough to me! (crazy, I know) At one point I remembered that last year we were at Sea World for the first 3 nights so we only stayed at Disney for 7 nights and that somehow made me feel better.

This trip will fall over Super Soap weekend which Olivia and I have been to twice before and really enjoy going to so we are excited about that but they made us wait till only about 2-3 days before the trip before they posted the schedule online. In the past it was posted a couple weeks ahead. They also announced that this is the final super soap weekend!! WHAT!! That ticks me off but then again I am glad we will get to be at the very last one. I warned Olivia that since our #1 guy (Jason from GH) won’t be there like he was last time we will not be getting up at 4am for this and we will not be spending 12 hour days at Super Soap. So do you think I held up to any of that ???? have to wait and see.

Ok so fast forwarding to the week of the trip. Alan has been going back and forth to Iowa for work. One week here one week there and the week that we are planning on leaving he is in Iowa. I didn’t really like that idea at all but he thought it was good because he would actually manage to get home early the day we leave. (that didn’t matter to me!)

We are leaving here on Thursday evening after the kids get out of school and Alan gets home from work. If he was at work in Decatur that would have been 5:30 but he had left Iowa on Wednesday after work and drove 3 hours before stopping for the night and then on Thursday he got up and drove the rest of the way and home. He had to unload in Decatur and return the rental car though so by the time he got here it was 2:30.

Well so departure day is Thursday but at almost midnight on Wed. night I had just finally went to bed after doing a bunch of stuff and I hadn’t been in bed more then 10-15 minutes and was almost asleep when I heard this weird noise. Now usually a weird noise would cause me to lay awake all night and think about what it was but I had been really busy all day and was very tired so I just ignored it. Then I heard it again and it sounded like a growl! Ok now I’m freaked out! I got up and made my way to the hall and saw the bathroom light was on and the door was shut! This was weird because I know Olivia wouldn’t bother to shut the door if going to the bathroom in the middle of the night and everyone who knows Owen has the bladder control of ?? But tonight I stand alone!

I cleaned Owen then I cleaned him again because yes it took twice to get the job done. Not counting that the next day I had to do it all over again because turns out that at almost 1:00am I must not have the best eye sight!
